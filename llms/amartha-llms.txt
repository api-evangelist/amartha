# Amartha

> Amartha (PT Amartha Mikro Fintek, operating as Amartha Financial Group) is an Indonesian
> technology company founded in 2010 that builds digital financial infrastructure for the
> grassroots economy, connecting rural micro-entrepreneurs — predominantly women running micro
> and small businesses — with affordable working capital from retail and institutional funders.

## Business lines (partner-facing)

- [Embedded Lending](https://amartha.com/en/bisnis/embedded-lending): Lets partner institutions,
  startups, and companies channel Amartha working-capital loans to their own users. No public
  integration reference is published; contact is via a form.
- [Institutional Investments](https://amartha.com/en/bisnis/institutional-investments): Loan
  channeling, joint financing, execution, syndicated lending, and revenue-sharing schemes for
  banks, non-bank financial institutions, and insurers. Entry point is a contact form.
